# R D Models: AI Search Visibility Plan (2026)

**Planning horizon:** 90 days, with 2027 resilience. **Operating assumption:** one real Jaipur business serving other cities through genuine projects and delivery, unless the owner verifies additional eligible staffed/customer-facing locations. **Evidence labels:** **PROVEN** = supported by official documentation or a clearly attributed study; **LIKELY** = practical inference, not a published ranking rule; **SPECULATIVE** = undocumented or untested hypothesis.

## A. Mechanism Map

### How an answer may be assembled

```text
Buyer query + language/location/context
                 |
                 v
   Search / retrieval eligibility
   crawl access -> index -> snippet/grounding eligibility
   [INFLUENCE: allow appropriate crawlers; fix technical/index issues]
                 |
                 v
 Query interpretation; sometimes query fan-out / follow-up searches
   [INFLUENCE: clear service, city, scale, materials and project facts]
                 |
                 v
 Candidate pages, business profiles and other sources retrieved
   [INFLUENCE: useful pages, accurate profiles, genuine independent evidence]
                 |
                 v
 Engine-specific selection, grounding and synthesis
   [PARTLY UNKNOWN: source weights, entity/passages, ordering, personalization]
                 |
                 v
 Answer, citations/links, map pack or voice response
   [MEASURE: answer inclusion, first-mention, accurate citation, qualified lead]
```

**PROVEN — Google:** AI Overviews (AIO) and AI Mode may use query fan-out; the exact subqueries and citation algorithm are not published. Pages need to be indexed and eligible for a Search snippet; Google documents no special AI schema or separate AI technical requirement. AIO appears when Google judges it adds value, and AIO and AI Mode can differ. Search Console reports their traffic under Web, with no general separate AI-citation report described in the cited guidance [1][23].

**PROVEN — other engines:** ChatGPT Search may rewrite prompts into multiple searches, can use more than one search provider and may use approximate location; OAI-SearchBot is the documented Search crawler control [10][11]. Gemini API grounding runs Google Search and returns source-linked annotations; that is not a full disclosure of the consumer Gemini app’s selection process [12]. Perplexity describes real-time web search and citations, uses its own and third-party crawlers, and says it respects robots.txt [13][14]. Copilot generates Bing queries and grounds from Bing’s crawl/index/ranking foundation [15][16]. Claude can search iteratively and cite; Anthropic documents separate crawler identities, but not a general-web index partner [17][18].

**LIKELY:** Crawlability, accurate identity, clear service descriptions, original work evidence, and corroboration on credible independent sites improve the chance of being understood and retrieved. These are foundations, not a first-place formula.

**SPECULATIVE:** A universal crawl → embeddings/vector database → fixed RAG pipeline; published passage-ranking gates, entity-salience thresholds, “E-E-A-T gating” for answer citations, special AI freshness boosts, or a controllable first-brand rule. Those internals are not established by the reviewed public documentation. Freshness systems exist for queries where freshness is expected, not a universal AIO advantage [3][4]. Schema, Wikidata, FAQ markup, `llms.txt`, or a particular conventional rank cannot force a citation [9][26].

**Injection boundary:** improve truthful source quality and eligibility; do not attempt to manipulate the answer-generation stage. Voice is an interface, not a separate reliable ranking system: test it as a distinct surface because results and output length can differ.

## J. Risks & 2027 Readiness

### Key risks and controls

- **Identity conflict:** address, domain and dates disagree publicly. **Control:** owner-approved source of truth, correct controlled profiles first, then request third-party corrections; preserve truthful history rather than silently inventing uniform dates [33][34].
- **Local distance constraint:** a Jaipur business cannot legitimately manufacture local proximity in every city. **Control:** describe true service coverage and publish real out-of-city project proof; do not pretend to have branches [5][6].
- **Policy risk:** doorway pages, scaled low-value content, manipulative links, fake reviews, misleading markup and false profiles can undermine visibility or trust. **Control:** content review, review policy, structured data matching visible facts, no bulk tactics [7][8][9][16].
- **Measurement noise:** prompt rewrites, location, personalization, model/UI changes and answer variability. **Control:** repeated standardized sampling, timestamps, exact prompts and saved outputs; compare trends, not anecdotes.
- **Tool/report limitations:** Search Console and vendor monitors may not expose every citation; Bing citation totals are not rank. **Control:** retain manual checks and business outcome data [23][24].
- **Crawler privacy and access:** blocking a search crawler can reduce search visibility; allowing a bot is not a security permission. **Control:** review each provider’s current crawler identity/purpose and protect private assets with authentication, not robots.txt [11][14][18].
- **Competitor narratives:** public listing or service page is not proof of an office or AI ranking. **Control:** use competitor facts only when the source establishes the specific claim; recheck time-sensitive data.

### 2027–2028 readiness

**PROVEN:** search is moving toward conversational, multi-query and more action-oriented experiences; Google announced conversational Maps exploration including India. AI commerce/agent announcements exist, but do not establish an advantage or ranking integration for bespoke architectural models [1][35][40].

**LIKELY:** buyers may increasingly compare project capabilities, location/service reach, lead time, evidence and contactability in conversational flows. Future-proof the business with clear structured facts, current portfolio, project permissions, accessible contact/quote path, prompt response, and trusted customer/industry references.

**SPECULATIVE:** universal procurement agents, fixed supplier shortlists, an India-wide agent protocol, or any specific attribute that will guarantee selection. Do not build a custom checkout/API for quote-dependent bespoke work until real customer demand supports it. Reassess crawler, profile, privacy and measurement documentation quarterly.
